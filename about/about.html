Biography: My name is Diana Rojas and I am a Mexican-American first generation college student from Morgan Hill California. I am studying Visual Communication Design and dream to study abroad. I enjoy fashion design, cake decorating, as well as web design! When I was younger all I wanted was to become a fashion designer and create beautiful wedding dresses. I later found a love for coding and was a computer science major during community college where I ultimately found the intersection of tech and design much more suitable for me. I also was a cake decorator for 7 years which I still love to do in my free time but that’s where I truly felt aligned with myself which meant I had a love for creativity. Not only am I taking this course to fulfill my major requirements but because I also want to design websites of my own and simply gain the knowledge of front-end development and apply that to my existing knowledge of back-end development and know the ins and outs of software development without losing the focus on the beauty on both sides of the spectrum.


This is my Tiktok "Portfolio" where I post videos of me cake decorating!

Reflection: I learned how to embed a youtube link using the iframe tag which I am curious to learn more about its purpose and abilities. I also learned to reference my external stylesheet and initially made a mistake of forgetting to include that and wondered why my CSS wasn’t applying to the document. So that was definitely a learning moment and I also found out that I can directly classify the size of my video box in html as I reference it. My video self portrait communicates my journey of discovering that I’ve always been creative and how despite my decisions to go another route I still found my way back to design and have found a perfect blend of both creativity and technology. I decided to leave out certain details that simply weren’t relevant to completing this assignment such as my other hobbies because I want the user to get to know my creative side and what I have accomplished with that as well as demonstrate that I have found a community of people who share the same passion for creativity by linking my tiktok “portfolio”. Similarly to brainstorming alt text, I wanted to only showcase what's important about me as a design student and no further personal details.
