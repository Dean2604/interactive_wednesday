Kimora Lee Simons


Baby Phat was created and launched in 1999 by fashion designer and model Kimora Lee Simmons and single handedly made a place for women streetwear in the early 2000’s. What inspires me the most is how Kimora not only created a clothing brand but successfully encapsulated the early 2000’s through her designs. Baby phat almost carries a feeling and is a time capsule for me. I remember watching her runway shows and growing up with an older sister who wore her clothes and I simply couldn’t wait to grow up and dress as vividly as I wanted. Her iconic cat symbol along with her use of velvet, animal print, and especially denim carries an audacious attitude that serves as a reminiscence of one of my favorite eras in fashion.


Jessica Villa



My older sister studied photography as well as graphic design and has grown to be one of my strongest inspirations. Watching her create and share her designs with me is what initially introduced me to this world of creatives. Seeing her succeed in this career was truly inspiring, especially being a first generation college student our parents weren’t thrilled to hear we wouldn’t become doctors or lawyers but she most definitely proved hem wrong. I wish to simply do the same and earn my place in the design world.


Corbin Sharpe



Corbin Sharpe is an incredible lasershow designer who's worked with edm artists such as Illenium, Crankdat, and my personal favorite Levity for their Lasership tour!I’ve always loved production and going to shows to not only listen to music but experience the light shows and graphics. Corbin programs the most colorfully intricate lights I’ve ever seen and maps them with the music so accurately it's a show on its own. I can only imagine what this process is like and how much equipment, software knowledge, and cues goes into executing an entire show. I now carry the same appreciation and admiration for the lighting team as I do the artist. I would love to learn more about light design and from attending these shows I can confidently say they're getting insanely getting better and better.
