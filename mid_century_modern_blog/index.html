Reflection: To be truthful, this assignment has been a complete nightmare however, I do feel my knowledge and approach getting stronger. I foolishly felt like I could replicate this blog confidently but was quickly humbled. I first started out with the easy stuff such as background colors and change of fonts but have been stuck on adding that orange side padding which I fear should've been added much earlier. My approach during this assignment involved doing the things I knew I could easily do and move onto the harder aspects however, I believe it's not an efficient approach because of the hierarchy, and as a result, certain elements are not conforming to my demands. I learned that classes are a true lifesaver and I also learned that they're also not that reliable at the same time. I still have some elements overriding my classes which has been extremely frustrating and have had to clean up and go backwards almost. I'm still trying to have my smaller images display horizontally but They are stacking on top of each other unlike the blueprint. What actually worked was my navigation format as well as the overall first page, but Once you scroll down is where hell broke loose and I lost control of the behaviour of certain elements and wasn't able to manipulate them the way I intended. I am glad that I got to try to replicate this page and really dive in and make mistakes I really admire this form of learning because it forces me to use what I have learned and find problems that definitely have solutions that i'll learn in future lessons.
