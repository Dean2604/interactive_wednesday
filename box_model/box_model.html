Reflection: This was super fun and easy-to-follow!! At first I didn't really know the difference between padding and margins but after changing the colors and sizes of both it made visualizing these content “boxes” much more easily. Padding is the space between the content and inside the border and margins refer to the space outside the border. I ran into trouble when adding borders. In other words, my borders weren’t visible even after inputting size and color parameters. After a little research I learned about the solid parameter which describes the style of the border which I didn’t include at first. That's why it wasn’t showing up and it makes sense because my dotted parameter was no issue so that was a learning moment. I also enjoyed learning about how to utilize the display property to stack my elements with the display:block; method and vice versa with the display:inline; method. I found these properties to be pretty straightforward and friendly to help organize my text in vertical or horizontal orientations.
